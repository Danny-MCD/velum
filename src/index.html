How Velum works

  1. Velum bundles Tor and runs it on your machine - nothing is sent to a server we run.
  2. When you publish a site, Velum generates a private key on your computer and asks Tor for a matching .onion address.
  3. Static folders are served by a tiny local web server inside Velum; "existing site" mode just points Tor at a port you're already running.
  4. Your address and key live in a local file on your machine. Back up the key if you want to reuse the same address elsewhere.
  5. Closing Velum stops Tor and takes your sites offline. Reopen it and hit Publish to bring them back.
